Using nuBuilder Next

Everyday work: signing in, finding your way, lists and records.

For: Everyone

nuBuilder Next is a modern front end for nuBuilder applications. It works on the same database as nuBuilder, so your forms, users and data are the same in both.

This chapter is for everyone who works with an application in nuBuilder Next: how to sign in, find your way, search lists and edit records. What you see depends on the rights your administrator gave you – menus, tiles and buttons you may not use are not shown.
