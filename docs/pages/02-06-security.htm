Your password and two-step login

Changing your password, setting up a second factor and signing in with a passkey.

For: Everyone

Open the Security tile of the start page (users without an admin role: the user menu).

Changing your password

Enter your current password and the new one twice. The rules – minimum length and, if your administrator set them, character classes – are listed below the field and ticked while you type; a password that appears in known data leaks is refused. Your administrator may also ask you to change the password after a while: a note after signing in says how many days are left, and an expired or leaked password must be changed right after signing in. Forgot it? Use Forgot password on the login page – you receive a link by e-mail that is valid for 60 minutes and can be used once.

Two-step login (2FA)

  • Authenticator app (Microsoft / Google Authenticator, …): scan the QR code and confirm with a code. From then on you enter the 6-digit code of the app after your password.
  • Passkey: after your password, Use a passkey – your device asks for its fingerprint, face or PIN instead of a code (see below).
  • Code by e-mail: if no app is set up, a 6-digit code is sent to your e-mail address.
  • Remember this device: no code is asked on this browser for a number of days.
  • Lost your phone? Ask your administrator to reset your two-step login.

Passkeys

A passkey signs you in with your device's fingerprint, face or PIN instead of a code. It is kept on this computer, your phone, a security key or in a password manager (then it is synced to your other devices), and it only works for the address of this site – a fake site cannot use it.

  • Add one: Security → Passkeys → a name (e.g. Laptop) → Add a passkey, then confirm on your device. You may have to confirm your password first. Add one per device you use.
  • Second step after the password: when your account needs two-step login, the second step asks for your passkey first; Use authenticator app and Send a code by e-mail instead stay available below it.
  • Without a password (when your administrator switched it on): Sign in with a passkey on the sign-in page, or pick the passkey in the list the user name field shows.
  • Confirming dangerous actions: where the password is asked again, Confirm with a passkey works too.
  • Remove one with its bin button. Lost a device? Remove its passkey from another device, or ask your administrator.
  • Passkeys need an https address; elsewhere the card says so.
