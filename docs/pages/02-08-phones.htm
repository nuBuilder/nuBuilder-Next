Phones and small screens

How nuBuilder Next adapts to phones and tablets, in one place.

For: Everyone

nuBuilder Next has no separate mobile version: every page adapts to the width of the screen. The main differences:

WhereOn a phone
Menu barWithout a logo (Branding): the icons stay in the bar, the areas move into the user menu (☰), and so do the theme and the language – at the top of that menu – so the bar fits also with an environment label. With a logo: the areas are behind the ☰ button, and the pin button next to it keeps the menu open on every page (until it is tapped again; kept per browser). The user button shows only its icon.
Record buttonsSave, Clone, New and Delete show only their icon (Save as a floppy disk); the text is the tooltip.
Header of form pagesInstead of the breadcrumb and the button row: a link ‹ <previous page> (the Back button). On a list the page title follows with the buttons as plain icons on its right (table / cards, Export, ⋮); on a record the title has a row of its own – up to two lines – and the buttons (Save, Clone, New, Delete, ⋮) sit as icons in the row below it. Add of a list sits next to the search field. The shared setting Back Button on Phones (Settings → Shared settings) shows the back link as ‹ only or hides it; on nuBuilder Next's own pages it does the same with their Back button.
Header of the other listsThe lists of nuBuilder Next (users, forms, procedures, reports, log …) look the same below 576 px: ← Back as a small link above, the title on the left and the buttons as plain icons on its right (Add, ⋮, table / cards, view, Export); the breadcrumb above the list is left out, Back leads to the same page. The bar keeps only Back, Add and the view menu; every other action (Export, Print, Import …) is an entry of the ⋮ menu, with Card or table at its top.
SettingsThe list of sections on the left becomes one selection field above the page (sections and their pages); the subtitle of the page is left out.
Touch screensFields of record pages keep their full size on tablets and phones; the smaller fields are used only with a mouse. Text inside fields is at least 16 px (an iPhone zooms the page into smaller fields – fields of a form with fixed positions only once they are stacked on a phone), and what can be tapped reacts on 24 px or more: (i) tips, the key mark, check boxes and switches, small buttons, filter chips. The editor's hint Press ⌥0 for help is left out.
TabsA row of tabs or sections that is wider than the screen scrolls sideways: the open one is moved into view and the cut edge fades. The tabs of a form that would take three rows or more on a phone become one such row.
Buttons on tabletsThe buttons of a form (Save, Clone, New, Delete, Design, Help …) show only their icon up to a width of 992 px; the label is the tooltip.
Lists as a tableA list shown as a table scrolls sideways; the first column stays and its title stays on top. The list keeps its sideways position when you sort, page, filter or reload it (per list, in this browser tab), and after a sort the column you sorted by is moved into view if it is not on screen.
Sub-formsBelow 576 px every row of a grid sub-form is a small card, the column titles as labels and the delete box with its own label. Table on phones in the properties of the sub-form object (design mode) keeps the table, which then scrolls sideways.
Getting readyThe bar at the top is left out on phones; the checklist's card on the start page stays. Two or more notices on the start page (renamed files, tutorial, Getting ready) share one strip that folds – folded on phones until it is opened; the choice is kept on the device.
Header of nuBuilder Next's own pagesEvery page starts the same way: the link ‹ <previous page>, then the title. It takes the place of the breadcrumb and of the Back button – also on pages with only one of them (the New form wizard, the documentation) and on lists whose Back sits in their card (translations, notifications, prompts).
Wide tablesBackups, library packs, the Compatibility check, formats and the cloners show each row as a card, every value with the title of its column.
TabletsSystem lists with more than five columns (users, forms, procedures …) show cards up to a width of 992 px, as on phones.
Designer tools of a formBelow 576 px Design, its entries (browse SQL, PHP, JavaScript, columns), the (i) form info and the layout switch (Absolute · Auto · Grid) leave the button row and sit at the top of the ⋮ menu – the row keeps the buttons every user needs.
Tabs with several cardsSuch a tab shows a small ▾. A tap on it – or a long tap on the tab (e.g. General of the user form) – lists its sections – Person, Organisation, Sign-in & access …; a tap jumps to that section. On a computer: right-click on the tab.
Start pageA layout without its own phone settings shows a one-column list, and tabs become the side bar. A layout can have separate settings for phone (narrower than 576 px), tablet (narrower than 992 px) and desktop – see Start page of the globeadmin.
Lists of nuBuilder NextBelow 768 px every row is a card (title, badge, up to four lines), or a compact table whose first column stays while the others scroll – set per list in its list view. A button next to the view menu switches between cards and table on your phone; the choice is kept on the device.
Form listsBelow 576 px every row is a card: the first column as its title, the others as Title: value lines; an empty value is left out with its title. A long value shows at most two lines and ends with …; tap the card to open the record with the whole text. Add shows only its + icon (on the system lists too). The ▦ / ☰ button next to it switches the list to a table (scrolling sideways) and back; the choice is kept per form on the device. No page-size choice (Back / Next stay). The column filters are folded behind the funnel button unless one is set. A list shown inside another form (a run object) gets a compact header, keeps its filters folded behind the funnel and grows to its rows.
Edit formsEvery form puts its objects below each other – also forms with fixed positions (nuBuilder Forte's pixel layout): below 576 px each field gets its label above it and the full width, in the order of their positions (top, then left); content boxes become section titles; HTML objects grow to their text, which wraps onto more lines there. Fields marked Fold on phones in design mode (on the Notes form: title, category and Updated on) fold into one row at the top when a saved record opens – the first value as its title, the others below; a tap opens them, and the choice is kept per form on the device. New records always show the fields. Text editors (TinyMCE) show no menu bar on phones and a short toolbar in one row that scrolls sideways – full screen, then undo, bold, italic, underline and lists first; Full screen on and off does not put the cursor into the text, so the on-screen keyboard stays closed; a toolbar the form's code sets is kept. Form code can keep the fixed layout with nuNextPhoneLayout(false) in nuOnLoad; nuBuilder Forte's nuPortraitScreen() turns this layout on at any width (capped at 720 px). The record appears once the form's code has run and the layout is done, so fields the code hides and the desktop positions do not flash up while the page loads.
DashboardsOne widget per row (tablets: two); table widgets show each row as a block (first column as title). A line chart over many days shows about six dates. In the dashboard editor each widget is a card.
TabletsFrom 768 to 991 px the fields of the automatic and grid layouts grow into the width of their row (none narrower than about 180 px), so selects and lookups are not cut. A form list with more than three column filters starts with them folded, as on phones – the funnel opens them. Design mode uses the bottom sheet of the phone up to 991 px, so the properties panel no longer covers the form.
nuBuilder Forte's system formsTheir list cards (Objects …) show the first five values; the rest is in the record.
User formThe summary at the top is compact: the initials beside the name, status and access level in one row (a long level name is cut – its tooltip shows it whole).
Code searchOne search row as on the start page: the field, Options and Filters (with the number set). Both open from the bottom of the screen; what is set shows as chips below the field. The keyboard's search key starts the search.
Admin rolesEach right is a row with its name and the choices None · View · Change as buttons.
Help icons and tooltipsTap instead of pointing.

On large screens (from about 1680 pixels) the content of a page sits in one framed sheet of at most 1600 pixels, centred on a slightly darker background, so the cards do not spread over the whole width; the top bar stays full width, but its logo and menus line up with the edges of the sheet. Design mode and the report designer keep the full width. A wider page width (per device, or for everyone in Settings → Look) changes this.

On wider screens forms built with absolute positions keep their positions; the automatic and grid layouts turn the same objects into responsive rows (see Form rendering pipeline), and content boxes with a direction arrange their objects per screen size – see Responsive forms.
