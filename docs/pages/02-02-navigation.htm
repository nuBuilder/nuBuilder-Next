Navigation and your start page

The menu bar, language and theme, your start page and the side bar.

For: Everyone

The top bar shows only the areas you have rights for. While Branding shows no logo, the bar is compact: a Home button comes first and the areas follow as links. With a logo, the logo opens the start page; Start is listed only next to other areas. How the bar looks on phones: Phones and small screens.

  • Language (Deutsch (DE), Deutsch (CH), English (UK), English (Australia), English (US), Français, Italiano, Español, Português, Русский, 简体中文, Nederlands, Polski, العربية): your choice is saved with your user. Only the languages your installation includes are offered (chosen by your administrator – Settings → General → Languages); with only one language the menu is hidden. Arabic is shown right to left. The three English versions differ in spelling (colour / color, customise / customize), the two German versions too (Deutsch (CH): ss instead of ß and «quotes»); the page language (<html lang>: de-DE, de-CH, en-GB, en-AU, en-US) follows the choice, e.g. for numbers in dashboards. nuBuilder Forte's formats (Formats) stay as they are. nuBuilder Forte (4.x) has only one English and one German, so the user's language stays English / German and the version is kept in the user's JSON (sus_json, NUNEXT_LANG_VARIANT). Without a choice: the installation's default (setup wizard), else the browser's (en-US, en-CA and plain "en" → US; en-AU, en-NZ → Australia; other English → UK; de-CH → Deutsch (CH), other German → Deutsch (DE)).
  • Back (, first button; on phones the ‹ link in the header): goes to the page you came from – from the log you opened on a user back to that user, from a record back to its list as you left it (same search and page). Searching, paging or saving on one page does not count as a step. Without a page before (a new browser tab, a bookmark) it goes one step up the breadcrumb instead: from a record to its list, from a list to the start page or the page above it (e.g. Procedures → Forms, Permission items → Users).
  • Breadcrumb (the path above the page title, e.g. Home / Settings / Branding): on a desktop without a logo in the top bar (Branding → Show logo) it moves into the top bar, right of the Home button.
  • Theme (sun / moon icon): light, dark or automatic (follows your device).
  • User menu (your name, top right): change password, this documentation (in a new tab), sign out; users without an admin role also find Security here – administrators open it from its start page tile, like System status.
  • F1 opens the page of this documentation that belongs to the page you are on, in a new tab – on form pages Editing records, on admin pages their own chapter. Pages without a matching chapter (sign-in, the documentation itself) leave F1 to the browser.
  • Reading level of this documentation (left, above the index): Essentials shows every topic with its main points and leaves out details and internals – … after a paragraph opens what was left out, in place; Standard shows the details; Everything adds the advanced topics. Your browser remembers the choice; a search or a link always finds a hidden passage.
  • Globeadmin: the start page shows all areas as tiles in groups; Customise changes what goes where, how the tiles look and which layout is used (see Start page of the globeadmin); + on a tile opens a new record, ▾ a menu with the pages behind it. The user menu also has the database tools (Database – phpMyAdmin / Adminer) and the File Manager.

Your start page

The start page shows tiles for the forms, reports and procedures your administrator set up for your access level. Customise → Appearance (key V) changes how the tiles look – only for you. Customise → Content → Personalise puts a and an on every tile: star your favourites (they appear in the row Favourites above the groups) and hide tiles you do not need; they are listed under Hidden by you while you personalise and come back with one click. Done saves, Cancel keeps everything as it was, Back to the standard removes your favourites and hidden tiles. The search field above the tiles filters them as you type (/ jumps into it; after the Home button or the logo of the top bar the cursor is already there).

Favourites and hiding by right-click: a right-click on a tile (a long press on a phone) shows its name and description and Add to favourites / Remove from favourites – where you may change the favourites of your start page (the globeadmin and admin roles on an editable layout, users on their own page). The same change as the ☆ in the side bar. Below it, Hide tile (admin start page) takes the tile off the page as Edit tiles and groups does – for everyone who uses that layout; on a user's start page Hide for me hides it for that user only, as Personalise does. A message with Undo follows, which puts the tile back in its place; later, Edit tiles and groups → Hidden or Personalise shows it again. The same menu opens on a tile in the search results (pages, forms, procedures or reports that are no tile here keep the browser's own menu); after the change the page reloads with your search still in the field.

The row Favourites above the groups shows your starred tiles as buttons whenever there are some (none starred – no row). A right-click on the row (a long press on a phone) offers Edit favourites – Customise with only the favourites shown – and Hide this row; on a chip these come below the chip's own entries. Appearance → Recently used shows the pages, forms and tiles you opened last as a row as well – it is the same list as in the side bar (kept per user on the server, on every device); ⊗ next to its heading empties it, and so do Clear recently used in the row's right-click menu and a right-click on the side bar's list; the menu can also hide the row. A right-click on one of its chips also offers Add to favourites (or Remove from favourites) when that entry is a tile of your start page. A hidden row comes back with Customise → Appearance → Favourites / Recently used – the message after hiding names the way and has Undo. A tap on the heading of a row folds it (▸, with the number of entries) or opens it again (▾); the state is remembered on this device. On a phone Recently used starts folded. Auto collapse in a row's right-click menu keeps that row folded whenever the page opens and folds it again after you pick one of its entries (per row, on this device).

Page numbers

Every page of nuBuilder Next has a number – handy to tell someone where to look. Type it into the search field of the start page or into Ctrl+K and press Enter: P110 opens the access levels (p110 and P 110 work too). A suffix opens a tab or section, e.g. P700.2 = Update → Backups. The hundreds are the areas: P0xx start and own account, P1xx users and access, P2xx development, P3xx data, P4xx communication and integration, P5xx logs, P6xx settings, P7xx system. A page you have no right to says so as usual; a number that does not exist brings you back with a message. Your own forms have no number – use their code.

Side bar: favourites and recently used

An optional column on the left of every page: Favourites – the tiles marked with ☆ on your start page, in the same order – and Recently used – the last 8 pages and forms you opened (a form counts once, whichever record). Switch it on or off for yourself on the start page: Customise → Appearance → Side bar; it is off until you or your administrator switch it on.

  • ☆ next to an entry makes a start page tile a favourite or stops it (the start page shows the same change). Forms that are not tiles of your start page have no ☆.
  • The side bar is a drawer on every screen: the button left of the logo opens it; ✕, Esc or a click beside it closes it.
  • Pin ( in its header, on screens from 992 px): the side bar stays open as a column next to the page, on every page, until you unpin it – with the pin again or the button left of the logo. The choice is yours (stored with your user, the same on every device); on narrower screens it stays a drawer.
  • A right-click on an entry of Recently used (a long press on a phone) → Remove from recently used takes that one out – in the side bar and in the start page's row. ⊗ next to Recently used – or a right-click on the list → Clear recently used – empties the list (also the start page's row). Entries you no longer have the right to open are not shown. The list is kept on the server per user (table zzzzsys_nunext_recent, the last 10), also while the side bar is off – the start page's row Recently used shows the same list; tiles opened on the start page that lead to no page of their own (reports, links, tools) count too. Demo visitors keep it for the session.
  • The globeadmin sets the default for everybody without an own choice: Settings → Look → Side bar for everyone by default. A user's own choice (in sus_json, key NUNEXT_SIDEBAR) wins.
