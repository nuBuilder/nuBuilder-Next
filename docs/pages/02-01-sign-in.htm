Signing in

Signing in, session time-outs and what happens when an account is locked or expired.

For: Everyone

Sign in with your nuBuilder user name and password. Depending on your access level you may be asked for a second factor (see Security).

If your session has ended (no activity for a while), the next click shows the sign-in page; after signing in you are taken to the page you had clicked – also when the sign-in page was left open for a long time.

  • Your account can expire or be locked by an administrator – then the login is refused.
  • If your administrator requires a new password, you are asked to set one right after signing in.
  • After a period without activity you are signed out automatically and must sign in again.
  • If your company uses the shared login, you are signed in automatically when you are already signed in to nuBuilder.
  • If the sign-in page shows Try the demo, one click signs you in to a demo: you can look at everything, but nothing you change is saved (a badge Demo – read only in the menu bar shows it).
