Branding

Logo, title and environment label.

For: Administrators (globeadmin, admin roles)

Admin tools → Branding (globeadmin) gives this installation its own look – on the sign-in pages and in the top bar:

  • Logo and optional logo for dark mode: PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF or SVG up to 512 KB, any aspect ratio (at most 32 px high in the top bar, 72 px on the sign-in pages). They are stored in nuBuilder Forte's file table zzzzsys_file (codes nunext_logo and nunext_logo_dark), so they are part of backups and App cloner copies and can also be replaced in Forms → Files. If a logo is deleted there, nuBuilder Next's logo is shown again. An SVG logo is always served with a sandbox policy, so script inside it cannot run.
  • Show logo: everywhere (top bar and sign-in pages), only on the sign-in pages, or nowhere – for the own logo and nuBuilder Next's. Without a logo in the top bar the title stands there when it is switched on (below); Home is then reached through the menu.
  • Show the title next to the logo: the title of nuBuilder Forte's settings ($nuConfigTitle) in the top bar.
  • Use the logo as browser icon instead of nuBuilder Next's icon (square logos work best).
  • Accent colour and the other colours are part of the themes (Admin tools → Themes); an accent colour set here before is kept as the own theme "My theme".
  • Environment label, e.g. TEST or DEV, with its own colour: a badge in the top bar and on the sign-in pages, a line of its colour at the top of every page and [TEST] in the browser tab title – so a test copy is not mistaken for the live system. $nuConfigEnvironmentLabel and $nuConfigEnvironmentColor in nuconfig.php take priority over the page (the field is then read-only): the label belongs to the server, and a database copied to a test server with the App cloner would otherwise bring the label of the server it came from. Example: $nuConfigEnvironmentLabel = "TEST"; $nuConfigEnvironmentColor = "#e67e22";
  • The preview on the page shows logo, title, colours and label before saving. Reset removes the logos and all branding settings. Error pages and the installer always use nuBuilder Next's look; a login page from $nuWelcomeBodyInnerHTML keeps its own HTML (accent colour and label apply). nuBuilder Forte (4.x) itself is not affected.
