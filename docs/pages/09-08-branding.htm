Branding

Logo, accent colour and environment label.

For: Administrators (globeadmin, admin roles)

Admin tools → Branding (globeadmin) gives this installation its own look – on the sign-in pages and in the top bar:

  • Logo and optional logo for dark mode: PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF or SVG up to 512 KB, any aspect ratio (at most 32 px high in the top bar, 72 px on the sign-in pages). They are stored in nuBuilder's file table zzzzsys_file (codes nunext_logo and nunext_logo_dark), so they are part of backups and App cloner copies and can also be replaced in Forms → Files. If a logo is deleted there, nuBuilder Next's logo is shown again. An SVG logo is always served with a sandbox policy, so script inside it cannot run.
  • Show the title next to the logo: the title of nuBuilder's settings ($nuConfigTitle) in the top bar.
  • Use the logo as browser icon instead of nuBuilder Next's icon (square logos work best).
  • Own accent colour: buttons, links, switches and checkboxes, active tabs, focus rings and highlights (Bootstrap's primary colour). Dark mode gets a lighter version; links are darkened automatically where they would be hard to read on white, and buttons get black text when the colour is very light. The colours come from a small generated stylesheet (the strict Content Security Policy allows no inline styles) that the browser caches until the next change.
  • Environment label, e.g. TEST or DEV, with its own colour: a badge in the top bar and on the sign-in pages, a line of its colour at the top of every page and [TEST] in the browser tab title – so a test copy is not mistaken for the live system. $nuConfigEnvironmentLabel and $nuConfigEnvironmentColor in nuconfig.php take priority over the page (the field is then read-only): the label belongs to the server, and a database copied to a test server with the App cloner would otherwise bring the label of the server it came from. Example: $nuConfigEnvironmentLabel = "TEST"; $nuConfigEnvironmentColor = "#e67e22";
  • The preview on the page shows logo, title, colours and label before saving. Reset removes the logos and all branding settings. Error pages and the installer always use nuBuilder Next's look; a login page from $nuWelcomeBodyInnerHTML keeps its own HTML (accent colour and label apply). nuBuilder itself is not affected.
