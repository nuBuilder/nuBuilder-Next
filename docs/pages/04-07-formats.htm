Formats

Date, number and text-mask formats shared with nuBuilder Forte (4.x).

For: Users with rights on: nuformat

Forms → Tools → Formats. Formats are shared with nuBuilder Forte (table zzzzsys_format).

When the pattern of a used format changes, the objects and list columns using it are updated. A format can only be deleted when it is unused. In the list, the Used number opens a menu of the forms using the format; a click opens the form (in design mode for form designers).

Typing or pasting numbers: a number field accepts numbers in other notations too – from Excel, calculators or PDFs – and shows them in its own format when you leave it; nothing is cleared.

  • Currency signs, letters, %, spaces (also no-break and thin spaces) and apostrophes are ignored.
  • Negative: a minus in front (also the calculator minus − and dashes –), a minus at the end (1.234,56-) or brackets (1.234,56).
  • Decimal separator: with both . and , the last one (1.234,56 and 1,234.56 are both 1234.56). With one kind only: several of them are thousands separators (1.234.567); a single one is the decimal separator when it is the field's own or is not followed by exactly three digits (1,5 = 1.5). Only 1,234 / 1.234 are ambiguous – the field decides: with , as its decimal separator 1,234 is 1.234, otherwise 1234.
  • Examples in a field 1,000.00: −1.234,56 → -1,234.56 · €1 000,50 → 1,000.50 · (1.234.567,89) → -1,234,567.89 · 1'234.50 → 1,234.50.
