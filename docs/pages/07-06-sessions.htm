Logged-in users

Advanced Active sessions and ending them.

For: Administrators (globeadmin, admin roles)

Log → Logged-in users lists every active nuBuilder Next session: user, IP address, browser, login time and last activity (green = active in the last 5 minutes); click a column title to sort by it (again = the other way round), newest activity first by default. The search field finds users by login name, display name or full name, in both lists (nuBuilder Next and nuBuilder Forte (4.x)); Log out all others and Log out everyone still mean every session. Activity is recorded at most every 30 seconds.

  • Filters next to the search: User and Status (active / idle for more than 5 minutes). On phones they are behind the funnel button next to the search (open at once while a filter is set). A user with several sessions is one row with the number of sessions – the chevron shows them; its End sessions ends all of them except your own. The user name opens the user record.
  • End session signs the user out with his next click (your own session cannot be ended here).
  • The Log out button above the list holds the three actions in its menu. Log out idle users: pick how long ago the last activity was (more than 1, 2, 4, 8 … hours – only times below the longest session timeout are offered, older sessions end by themselves) and confirm: every nuBuilder Next session idle for longer is ended, your own stays. Written to the log.
  • Log out all others ends every nuBuilder Next session except your own; Log out everyone (incl. me) ends your own session too, and you sign in again. Both ask first and are logged (All sessions ended). nuBuilder Forte's own sessions are not touched.
  • The (i) next to the title shows the current timeouts. Sessions without activity for longer than $nuConfigSessionTimeout (globeadmin: $nuConfigSessionTimeoutGlobeadmin) disappear.
  • The second list shows nuBuilder Forte's sessions (zzzzsys_session) for information – folded, with their number; click its title to open it. They are ended in nuBuilder Forte.
